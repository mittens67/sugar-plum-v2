# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Primary customers are people looking for sweet treats and a delightful shopping experience. They are often in a "hungry" or "treat-seeking" state of mind and appreciate a sense of magic and coziness.

## Product Purpose
Sugar Plum v2 is a magical e-commerce platform for an artisanal bakery. It exists to showcase high-quality pastries and cakes in a way that feels enchanting and appetizing, moving beyond a generic shopping cart experience.

## Brand Personality
Whimsical, Artisanal, Magical. The brand is sweet, cozy, and evokes a sense of "delicious wonder."

## Anti-references
- Sleek/Clinical: No cold, sharp, or overly modern minimalist designs.
- Monochrome: Avoid desaturated or purely black-and-white palettes.
- Generic SaaS: No boring cards, flat grids, or corporate-style iconography.

## Design Principles
- **Sensory Delight**: Every element should aim to make the user "hungry." Use warm colors, soft textures, and fluid motion.
- **Whimsical Craft**: Avoid generic patterns. Interactions should feel handcrafted and slightly magical (e.g., subtle sparkles, soft transitions).
- **Cozy Abundance**: The UI should feel "full" and welcoming, like a bakery display case, rather than sparse and clinical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.
- Support for reduced motion (important for a whimsical site with animations).
- High contrast for appetizing text overlays.
